# Toddler Space: A keyboard smashing game for toddlers

**Play it here: https://arshad-kamal.github.io/toddler-space/**

It runs in the browser, needs nothing installed, and works offline once the page has loaded.

**Play:** open the page in Chrome or Edge on a computer, choose a planet, and let little hands loose on the keyboard.

- **Smash** (violet planet): every key makes something happen. Letters and numbers pop up big and are spoken, Space makes a supernova, Enter a planet, the arrows comets, and the other keys send rockets, UFOs, black holes, meteor showers and constellations. Simple words like CAT or SUN come alive with a picture.
- **Words** (teal planet): calm spelling. Letters land in glowing tiles; press Space or Enter when the word is done. A real word is read out and earns a star; many show their picture. The game keeps the best score on this device.

Tap **Change name** on the start screen to put your child's name in the title. The name, the best score, the two switches and the **Effects** level (High, Medium or Low: fewer sparkles, rings and flashes on a calmer screen or a slower computer) are kept in this browser only; nothing is sent anywhere.

## Grown-ups

- In Chrome or Edge on a computer the game goes full screen and locks the keyboard, so the Windows key, Alt+Tab and Ctrl+W can't close it. **To get out, hold Esc for 2 seconds**, then click **All done**.
- Windows keeps a few combinations no web page can take, such as Ctrl+Alt+Del and Windows+L. In Settings → Accessibility → Keyboard, turning off the Sticky Keys and Filter Keys shortcuts stops their pop-ups.
- Firefox and Safari can't lock the keyboard; the game still plays.
- On phones and tablets, tap a planet and the keyboard opens; if it closes, tap the **Keyboard** button.
